Built Forward Because Others Shared Back

To the builders who left the door open.

LeeWay did not arrive in a vacuum. It was built in a world made richer by engineers, researchers, designers, maintainers, standards communities, and open-source contributors who chose to publish what they learned and give the next builder somewhere real to begin.

To every person who wrote a library, documented a protocol, reviewed a pull request, fixed a regression, maintained a package after midnight, answered an issue from a stranger, published a model, released a design system, or placed years of hard-earned engineering behind a license that allowed somebody else to learn from it: thank you.

LeeWay has studied, depended on, synchronized, tested against, adapted around, and learned from an extraordinary range of public work. Some projects provided direct foundations. Some provided tools. Some taught us a design pattern, an execution model, a transport mechanism, a map, a browser harness, a device strategy, or simply a better way to think about the problem.

We especially recognize the maintainers and communities who chose licenses such as MIT, Apache 2.0, ISC, and other open or openly shared terms. Those choices turn individual engineering into a ladder other people can climb. We intend to honor that generosity with clear provenance, preserved upstream identity, honest attribution, and evidence that distinguishes what LeeWay created from what LeeWay learned from or built upon.

— Leonard Lee, Creator · LeeWay Industries

FOUNDATIONAL SPATIAL LINEAGE

God's Eye View · Bilawal Sidhu

LeeWay Logistics explicitly records God's Eye View as its upstream spatial lineage. Its work helped establish a serious starting point for spatial intelligence, globe interaction, live layers, and the idea that a map can become an operational window into the world.

This acknowledgment is not an attempt to absorb that identity into LeeWay. It is the opposite: a permanent statement that the upstream project and its creator deserve to remain visible in the history of what followed.
