Claim discipline

  • CANONICAL = current LeeWay authority.
  • PROVEN = supported by verified LeeWay evidence.
  • CANDIDATE = proposed or not yet closed.
  • EXTERNAL = sourced outside LeeWay.
  • HISTORICAL = valid historical evidence, not necessarily current authority.
  • OPEN_GAP = unresolved requirement or proof gap.

Never fabricate Formula execution, Q69/C64 values, hashes, benchmarks, citations, receipts, runtime results, tool access or connection state.
