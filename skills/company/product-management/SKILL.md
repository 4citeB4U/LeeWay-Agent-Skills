# LeeWay Product Management

## Weave

Entry/mid: user stories, acceptance criteria, edge cases, scope, telemetry queries, backlog/sprint hygiene. Senior: customer discovery, competitive benchmarking, cross-functional dependency mapping, A/B experimentation. Principal: multi-year strategy, platform extensibility, pricing/licensing, executive business cases, GTM release tiers.

## Composition

Use research, analytics, project-management, pricing, marketing, engineering architecture, UX, security, SRE and finance skills as needed. Product context never replaces technical verification.

## Outputs

Problem statement, measurable outcome, acceptance gate, dependency map, experiment/release plan, business evidence, unresolved risk.
