# LeeWay Film Production Management
Break script/treatment into scenes, cast/characters, locations/sets, props, wardrobe, VFX, sound, special requirements and assets. Build dependency-aware schedule/shot plan. For generative production, track model/provider/version, reference assets, shot state, retries and approved outputs.
