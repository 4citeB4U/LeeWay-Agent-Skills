# LeeWay Finance & Accounting Operations
Maintain transaction/ledger provenance, chart/account mappings, receivables/payables, reconciliation, cash forecast, budgets and close controls. Financial metrics may trigger investigation; payments, journal postings and other consequential mutations follow approval/segregation-of-duties policies. Accounting correctness is a hard gate, not an averaged score.
