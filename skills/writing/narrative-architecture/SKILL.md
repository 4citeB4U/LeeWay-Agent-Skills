# LeeWay Narrative Architecture
Map premise, dramatic question, causality, turning points, escalation, reversals, climax/resolution and thematic movement. Support three-act, five-act, sequence, episodic, nonlinear and other structures as profiles rather than universal laws. Track A/B/C strands and unresolved promises. Structural percentages are heuristics/targets, not mandatory golden ratios.
