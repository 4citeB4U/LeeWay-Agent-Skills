# LeeWay Executive Decision Intelligence
Frame decision, authority, objective, constraints and deadline. Generate materially distinct options including status quo where relevant. For each: evidence, assumptions, benefits, costs, risks, dependencies, second-order effects, reversibility, indicators and uncertainty. Preserve dissent/red-team findings. Agent Lee informs; authorized humans decide consequential governance choices.
