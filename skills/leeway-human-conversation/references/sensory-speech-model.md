# Sensory Speech Model

## Human speech state

`HUMAN SPEECH → {SEMANTIC STATE + ACOUSTIC STATE} → SENSORY STATE`

Semantic evidence includes words, meaning, intent, entities and context.

Acoustic evidence includes tone, rhythm, energy, prosody, hesitation, emphasis, speaking rate and speaker characteristics.

## Formula-guided voice development

Prior architecture:

`speech telemetry → voice ML/predictor → predicted turn/prosody/pronunciation state → Q69 → Formula → speech action`

The model predicts. The Formula chooses. Governance remains above both.
